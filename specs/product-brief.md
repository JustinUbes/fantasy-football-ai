# Product Brief

## Vision

An AI-powered fantasy sports analysis assistant would help users take their fantasy sports game up to the next level. It will act as a draft expert and an assistant throughout one's fantasy sports year, recommending lineup changes, pickups and whatever other useful information we find a useful use case for. The app will link with the users actual fantasy team either by fantasy app API or by user's manual addition for apps which do not have an API publicly exposed.

## Target Users

Fantasy sports players who are serious about their teams and seasons. Most people who play fantasy sports play in paid leagues and the idea of winning the pot will entice them to use AI and professional analysis to secure the money.

## Core Jobs To Be Done

Who to draft and when, who to pickup and when, weak spots and strong spots on a roster, matchup specific advice as well as reports the week after. 

## Success Criteria

A local program that acts as an advisor and librarian of the finest sports knowledge which drives users to successful, winning fantasy seasons.

## Constraints

We worry about cost, standing up an app like this will cost a lot, I am one person. Certain fantasy sites do not expose data, we will have to get creative for a seamless user experience.
